# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other protected characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, cultural authority, consent, and community governance.
- Respect privacy and the boundaries of research participants and performers.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable in
project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route on the
maintainer's [ORCID record](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-7904-3892). Reports will be
handled with discretion. Maintainers may correct, warn, restrict, or exclude
participants in proportion to the behaviour and its impact. Good-faith reports must
not be retaliated against.
